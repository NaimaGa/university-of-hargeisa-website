University Portals and Online Services

Centralized gateway for students, academic staff, and researchers. Sign in to access course registration, grade management, electronic library repositories, and campus digital services.

Student Academic Portal

Enrolled undergraduate and graduate students can view exam results, manage semester course selections, review tuition statements, and download unofficial transcripts.

Portal Services:
  • Semester Course Registration
  • Examination Timetables and Grades
  • Fee Balances and Payment Slips
  • Faculty Advisory Messaging

Staff and Faculty Portal

Authorized academic faculty and administrative personnel can enter semester grade rosters, upload course syllabi, verify student attendance, and submit institutional leave requests.

Portal Services:
  • Class Roster and Grade Submission
  • Curriculum Syllabus Uploads
  • Human Resources and Payroll
  • Institutional Committee Minutes

Digital E-Library Portal

Direct access to thousands of electronic books, peer-reviewed medical and scientific journals, legal databases, and digitized Somali historical manuscripts.

Included Repositories:
  • Research4Life Medical / Health Collections
  • JSTOR and ScienceDirect Scholarly Databases
  • University Theses and Dissertation Archive
  • Gaarood Institute Somali Oral Archives

Need Technical Assistance with Your Portal Account?

If you encounter difficulties accessing your student or staff account, please contact the University ICT Directorate via email at ict@uoh.edu.so or visit the Central Computing Centre at the Main Pepsi Campus with your valid university identification card.
