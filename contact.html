Visiting Hargeisa

Campus Locations and Transit Guide

How to locate and navigate our campuses throughout Hargeisa.

Main Pepsi Campus

Located along the main University Road in the Pepsi Area of Ahmed Dhagah District. Accessible via central public minibuses and taxi terminals departing from Downtown Hargeisa.

Ayaha Medical Campus

Situated in eastern Hargeisa within the Ayaha sector, directly connected by paved municipal roadways to Hargeisa Group Hospital clinical teaching grounds.

Agricultural Field Station

Positioned along the agricultural perimeter of Hargeisa, housing field research demonstration plots, meteorological monitoring equipment, and veterinary diagnostics.
