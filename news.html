Campus News and Events

Stay up to date with institutional developments, academic conferences, student achievements, graduation celebrations, and community initiatives at the University of Hargeisa.

October 2026 Campus Life

University Holds Freshmen Orientation and Welcome Assembly for New Students

The University of Hargeisa warmly welcomed its newest cohort of undergraduate students during the annual orientation week. University leadership, collegiate deans, and student union representatives delivered informative briefings detailing academic regulations, library facilities, laboratory protocols, and campus culture.

Incoming students participated in campus walking tours across the central Pepsi campus and met individually with faculty advisers to finalize their semester course schedules.

Recent Developments

Latest University Dispatches

Reporting on institutional milestones, scientific collaboration, and faculty achievements.

October 2026 Partnerships

University and Somaliland Quality Control Commission Sign Strategic Partnership

A memorandum of understanding was signed between the University of Hargeisa and the Somaliland Quality Control Commission to establish collaborative laboratory testing frameworks, food safety certification courses, and shared scientific research.

October 2026 Academics

Faculty Development and Academic Excellence Workshop Concludes

Lecturers and professors from across all seven constituent colleges gathered for an intensive three-day symposium focused on interactive teaching methodologies, digital learning platforms, and thesis supervision standards.

September 2026 Commencement

Annual Commencement Exercises Honor Thousands of New Graduates

In a grand ceremony attended by state dignitaries, university trustees, and families, the University conferred degrees upon graduates across undergraduate medicine, engineering, law, business, education, and master's programmes.

May 2026 Community

University Reaches Agreement Expanding Tuition Assistance for Disadvantaged Students

Demonstrating its commitment to social inclusion, university administrators concluded an agreement with community stakeholders to expand need-based tuition waivers and establish subsidized campus meal vouchers for students in need.

April 2026 Campus Facilities

Central University Library Expands Digital Research Terminals and Quiet Study Wings

Renovations to the main campus library have added fifty modern computer terminals, high-speed fiber connectivity, and subscriptions to leading international scholarly journals in medicine, law, and engineering.

March 2026 Infrastructure

University Inaugurates Modern Solar Energy Array at Central Pepsi Campus

In accordance with its sustainable environmental commitments, the university completed installation of a 150 kW photovoltaic solar system providing clean, reliable electricity for lecture halls, laboratories, and computing suites.

February 2026 Research

Annual Faculty Research Symposium Showcases Pioneering Scientific Work

Researchers from every college presented original studies in public health, climate adaptation, information systems, and Somali oral archives. The event drew scholars from partner universities across the Horn of Africa and attracted international research funding inquiries.
