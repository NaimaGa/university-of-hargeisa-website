Undergraduate Studies

Undergraduate Admissions

The University of Hargeisa invites applications from secondary school graduates who demonstrate strong scholastic achievement, integrity, and ambition.

Candidates may choose between full-time morning sessions, afternoon modules, and flexible evening classes designed to accommodate working students across all four city campuses.

Eligibility and Criteria:

  • Somaliland Secondary Certificate: Recognized certificate issued by the Ministry of Education with satisfactory marks in relevant subject clusters.
  • International Qualifications: IGCSE, A-Levels, International Baccalaureate, or certified regional equivalents authenticated by the Somaliland Quality Control Commission.
  • Programme Specific Minimums: Medicine (Grade A or minimum 85%), Engineering (Grade B+ or 75%), Business and Law (Grade C+ or 65%).
  • Entrance Testing: Candidates for competitive faculties undertake an aptitude assessment in English, Mathematics, and foundational sciences.
Postgraduate Studies

Graduate Admissions and Scholarships

The School of Graduate Studies offers specialized Master of Science, Master of Arts, and doctoral programmes aimed at professional advancement and specialized scientific research.

Postgraduate Entry Requirements:

  • An accredited Bachelor of Science or Bachelor of Arts degree from a recognized institution with a cumulative grade point average of at least 2.75 out of 4.0.
  • Official authenticated university academic transcripts and certified degree certificate.
  • Two formal academic or professional letters of reference attesting to intellectual suitability.
  • A comprehensive Statement of Purpose detailing research intentions and career objectives.

Scholarship and Financial Assistance Opportunities

Merit-based tuition scholarships are granted to the top graduating seniors from each college. Additionally, community need-based assistance supports talented students from economically disadvantaged rural areas.

Application Procedure

How to Apply to the University

Follow these four streamlined steps to complete your registration and secure your place in the upcoming academic semester.

01

Create Online Profile

Register through the University Portal, select your desired degree major, and enter your personal details.

02

Submit Documents

Upload clear digital copies of your secondary certificate, national identity card or passport, and recent photographs.

03

Assessment and Interview

Sit for the college aptitude examination and attend the faculty advisory interview at the main campus.

04

Orientation and Enrolment

Receive your formal Letter of Admission, complete tuition registration, and participate in campus orientation week.

Helpful Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Key information for prospective undergraduates, graduate applicants, and international students.

When do admissions open each year?

Admissions open twice annually: the primary Fall Semester intake begins application processing in July for classes starting in September. The secondary Spring Semester intake accepts applications in December for classes commencing in February.

Can I transfer credits from another university?

Yes. Students seeking credit transfer must submit authenticated transcripts and official course syllabi. The collegiate Academic Committee evaluates equivalence; transferred credits may comprise up to 40% of degree requirements.

Are evening classes available for professionals?

Yes. The College of Business, College of Law, and the School of Graduate Studies offer comprehensive evening schedules (5:00 PM to 8:30 PM) and Friday/Saturday weekend modules tailored for full-time working professionals.

How do international students obtain visa support?

Upon acceptance and payment of the registration deposit, the university International Student Office provides official sponsorship letters to facilitate student visa processing with the Somaliland Immigration Department.
