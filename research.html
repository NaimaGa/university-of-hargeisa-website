Research and Innovation

Advancing scientific inquiry, evidence-based policy formulation, and technological innovation to solve critical societal challenges in Somaliland and the Horn of Africa.

Research Priorities Research Committee Grants and Funding Publications and Journals Partnerships
Strategic Agenda

Institutional Research Priority Areas

Aligning university scientific output with national development plans and global sustainable development objectives.

Public Health and Epidemiology

Investigating maternal and child mortality reduction, infectious disease surveillance, waterborne pathogen diagnostics, and primary health infrastructure resilience in pastoral communities.

Climate Adaptation and Drylands

Developing drought-resistant sorghum varieties, rangeland rehabilitation methodologies, sand dam water harvesting, and renewable solar power mini-grids for off-grid settlements.

Governance, Law, and Customary Xeer

Documenting the interplay between traditional customary law (Xeer), Islamic jurisprudence, and modern statutory constitutionalism in commercial adjudication and peacebuilding.

Macroeconomics and Financial Systems

Research on mobile money adoption, remittance flows, inflationary trends, port logistics optimization at Berbera, and regional trade integration across the Horn of Africa.

Information Systems and AI

Natural language processing for Somali dialect translation, automated agricultural pest detection, and secure digital archiving of historical land registry records.

Somali Heritage and Oral Archives

Digitizing rare audio recordings of classic Somali poets, folklore preservation, historical cartography, and archaeological site documentation in collaboration with Gaarood Institute.

Governance and Ethics

The Directorate of Research and Ethics Committee

Ensuring methodological rigor, ethical standards, and legal compliance across all university research.

Directorate of Research and Community Services (DRCS)

The DRCS serves as the central administrative hub managing university research grants, academic conferences, intellectual property guidance, and publication dissemination.

  • Coordinates competitive annual faculty research grant cycles
  • Maintains the institutional repository and publication index
  • Negotiates memorandums of understanding with international research consortia
  • Provides statistical analysis training and research software licenses for faculty

Institutional Review Board (IRB)

The University of Hargeisa IRB reviews all research proposals involving human participants, patient medical records, vulnerable populations, or environmental impact assessments.

Ethical Clearance Certificate

All faculty members, postgraduate scholars, and visiting international researchers must obtain formal IRB ethical clearance prior to commencing field data collection.

Scholarly Output

Publications and Peer-Reviewed Journals

Disseminating verified scientific findings, legal analyses, and socio-economic evaluations.

Hargeisa Science Journal

A peer-reviewed biannual publication highlighting original empirical papers in public health, civil engineering, agronomy, environmental sciences, and computer software architecture.

Somaliland Law Review

Published by the College of Law, examining constitutional court rulings, statutory codification, customary Xeer harmonization, and maritime jurisdiction in the Gulf of Aden.

Policy Brief Series

Succinct, actionable evidence summaries delivered directly to governmental ministries, the Somaliland Parliament, regional health authorities, and international development agencies.

Collaborative Network

International Academic Partnerships

The University of Hargeisa collaborates with prestigious higher education institutions, United Nations agencies, and scientific bodies across Africa, Europe, North America, and Asia.

Partnership activities include faculty exchange programmes, joint degree curricula, reciprocal library access, and co-authored research grants. Through these global bridges, our scholars ensure that Somaliland perspectives are represented in worldwide academic discourse.
