About the University

Somaliland's Premier
Public University

The University of Hargeisa is a public higher educational institution located in Hargeisa, Somaliland. Established in 1998, academic operations commenced in 2000 with an initial cohort and two founding faculties.

Today it comprises 7 Colleges, 16 Faculties, and 37 undergraduate programmes, complemented by a School of Graduate Studies offering 35 postgraduate programmes. Operating across four campuses, the University of Hargeisa stands as the foremost chartered public university in Somaliland.

To be a world-class university that produces competent, ethical, and innovative graduates who contribute to the sustainable socio-economic development of Somaliland, the Horn of Africa, and the global community.

To provide quality higher education, research, and community service that meets international standards, fosters critical inquiry, and cultivates ethical leadership — empowering graduates to excel in an evolving global landscape.

The University Senate is the supreme academic governing authority of the institution. It directs academic policy, degree qualifications, examinations, and institutional quality assurance, ensuring rigorous standards aligned with international best practices.
